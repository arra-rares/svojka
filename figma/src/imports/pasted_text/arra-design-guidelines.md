# **ARRA — Landing Page Design Guidelines (v1)**

---

## **1. Frame & Layout**

* **Base width:** 390 px (mobile-first)
* **Max content width (desktop):** 1100–1200 px
* **Background:** `#FFFFFF`
* **Section spacing:** 64 px desktop / 40 px mobile
* **Horizontal padding:** 16 px mobile / 24 px desktop
* **Grid:** simple vertical flow (no complex grids)

### Rules

* Layout must be **linear and scannable**
* No nested complexity
* Each section = **one purpose only**
* No sidebars, no distractions

---

## **2. Typography**

Use **system fonts only**.

* iOS → SF Pro
* Android → Roboto
* Web → system-ui stack

---

### Sizes

* **32 px Bold** → Hero headline
* **22 px Semibold** → Section titles
* **18 px Medium** → Important labels (pricing highlights)
* **16 px Regular** → Body
* **14 px Regular** → Secondary
* **12 px Medium** → Small labels

---

### Colors

* **Primary:** `#111111`
* **Secondary:** `#6B6B6B`
* **Muted:** `#9E9E9E`

---

### Rules

* Max **2 font weights per section**
* No decorative typography
* No uppercase blocks except labels

---

## **3. Color System**

Minimal. Functional.

---

### Base

* Background: `#FFFFFF`
* Surface: `#FFFFFF`
* Divider: `#EAEAEA`

---

### Accent

* **Primary accent:** `#000000` (yes, black — safer for premium)
* **Action accent (CTA):** `#111111`
* **Optional highlight:** subtle gold/beige (`#C8A97E`) — very limited

---

### Rules

* No gradients
* No bright colors
* No color-coding for sections
* Photos provide visual richness, not UI colors

---

## **4. Imagery (Critical)**

This is the core of the product.

---

### Rules

* Images must carry **80% of the message**
* Always use **real event photos**
* Prefer:

  * people interacting
  * emotion
  * printed photos in hand
* Avoid:

  * empty setups
  * staged/artificial shots

---

### Treatment

* Full-width hero image
* Section images:

  * 4:5 or 1:1 ratio
* No filters
* Slight contrast boost allowed

---

## **5. Navigation**

Keep it almost invisible.

---

### Header

* Height: 56–64 px
* Left: logo
* Right: language switch

No menu unless strictly needed.

---

### Rules

* No dropdowns
* No complex navigation
* Page is the navigation

---

## **6. CTA System (Most Important)**

There are only **two actions**:

1. **Check availability (primary)**
2. **WhatsApp (secondary, floating)**

---

### Primary CTA

* Style:

  * background: `#111111`
  * text: white
  * radius: 12 px
* Height: 48–56 px

---

### Placement

* Hero (above fold)
* After pricing
* Final section

---

### Floating WhatsApp

* Bottom-right
* Always visible
* Small, non-intrusive

---

### Rules

* Never more than 1 primary CTA per screen
* No competing actions
* No “Learn more” nonsense

---

## **7. Sections — Visual Rules**

Aligned with structure from vision 

---

### Hero

* Full-width image/video
* Overlay gradient (very subtle)
* Text max:

  * 1 headline
  * 1 line subtext
* CTA visible immediately

---

### Social Proof

* Numbers + short testimonials
* No long paragraphs

---

### Services

* Cards or rows
* Each:

  * image
  * title
  * 1 line

---

### Pricing

Most sensitive section.

---

#### Layout

* Clear blocks
* No toggles
* No hidden info

---

#### Rules

* Prices must be readable in **<3 seconds**
* Highlight:

  * **3h → most popular**
* Show discount inline:

  ```
  120€ (60€ with Fotobox/360)
  ```

---

### How It Works

* 3–4 steps
* Icons optional
* Keep stupid simple

---

### Gallery

* Grid only
* No fancy layout
* Click → password → redirect

---

### CTA Section

* Strong contrast
* Short message
* Single button

---

### Contact

* Icons + links
* WhatsApp prominent

---

## **8. Forms**

---

### Fields

* date
* location
* event type
* number of people

---

### Rules

* Single column
* No multi-step
* No validation noise
* Fast submission

---

### Feedback

```
Request received. We will confirm within 24h.
```

---

## **9. Interaction Rules**

* No animations
* No transitions except basic hover
* No parallax
* No scroll tricks

Reason: speed + predictability 

---

## **10. Language Handling**

* `/sk`, `/en`, `/de`
* Same layout for all
* No text overflow hacks

---

## **11. Performance Constraints**

* Page load < 2s mobile
* Images optimized aggressively
* Lazy load below fold

---

## **12. Tone (Design + Content)**

From customer + positioning 

---

### Must feel:

* premium, but not expensive
* clean, but not sterile
* emotional, not corporate

---

### Avoid:

* agency-style design
* startup SaaS look
* wedding cliché overload

---

## **13. Hard Constraints**

From battle plan 

* no CMS
* no booking system
* no animations
* no feature creep

---

## **14. Guiding Principle**

If you’re unsure:

> Remove it.

Clarity > creativity
Conversion > aesthetics
